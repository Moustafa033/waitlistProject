Waitlist privacy notice

Version: waitlist-v1. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

1. Who we are

RabbitMove, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 42160066, the Netherlands. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for privacy questions: create@creatinginpublic.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • The language of your browser (for example "en" or "nl"), so we can write to you in the right language.
  • An optional campaign code from the link you used (for example "instagram"), never anything that identifies you.
  • Proof of your consent: which version of this text you agreed to, and when you signed up, confirmed or unsubscribed.

We do not collect your name or phone number, we do not store your IP address in our database, and this page uses no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Launch emails (a few emails at most about the launch of Brand AI): your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You give it by ticking the box and pressing the button in our confirmation email.
  • Keeping proof of consent and a do-not-email list after you unsubscribe, bounce or ask for deletion: our legal obligation to be able to prove consent and our legitimate interest in never emailing you again (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).
  • Protecting the form against bots and abuse (see Cloudflare Turnstile below): our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and stopping our form from being used to flood other people's inboxes (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Joining the waitlist is voluntary. If you do not give consent, we do not store your email.

4. Who processes data for us

We use these service providers, each under a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase (database and server functions), region: Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Resend (sends the confirmation and launch emails). Emails are sent from Ireland (EU); Resend stores account data and email logs in the United States.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (bot check on the form). To tell people from bots, Cloudflare processes technical data from your browser, including your IP address. We never receive or store that data. See Cloudflare's privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • Hostinger (web hosting of this website), which, like every web server, briefly processes your IP address to deliver the page.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU, it is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long we keep it

  • Never confirmed: deleted automatically 30 days after our last confirmation email.
  • Confirmed: kept until we have sent the launch emails, then deleted (unless you later agree to receive more).
  • Unsubscribed: deleted 12 months after you unsubscribe.
  • "Delete my data": deleted immediately.
  • After deletion we keep only a one-way keyed fingerprint (a hash) of your address on a do-not-email list, so we can keep the promise never to email you again. It cannot be turned back into your address.

6. Your rights

  • Withdraw your consent at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email. This does not affect emails sent before you withdrew.
  • Delete your data yourself, at any time, with the "Delete my data" button behind the unsubscribe link in our emails. It works immediately; no need to write to us.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use, to receive a copy of it, or to object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Write to create@creatinginpublic.com. We answer within one month.
  • Complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens: https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl

We do not sell your data, and we make no automated decisions about you.

7. Age

The waitlist is only for people aged 16 or older.

8. Security

Your address is stored in a locked database that the website itself cannot read, every link we send is single-use or signed, and only the people who run Brand AI can access the list.

9. Changes

If we change what we do with your data, we update this notice and its version number, and we ask for new consent where the law requires it.
