3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Launch emails (a few emails at most about the launch of Brand AI): your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You give it by ticking the box and pressing the button in our confirmation email.
  • Keeping proof of consent and a do-not-email list after you unsubscribe, bounce or ask for deletion: our legal obligation to be able to prove consent and our legitimate interest in never emailing you again (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).
  • Protecting the form against bots and abuse (see Cloudflare Turnstile below): our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and stopping our form from being used to flood other people's inboxes (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Joining the waitlist is voluntary. If you do not give consent, we do not store your email.

If we change what we do with your data, we update this notice and its version number, and we ask for new consent where the law requires it.
