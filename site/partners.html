FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need a big following?

No. We look for people whose community trusts them. Just starting out is fine if your training is real.

Is it paid?

Yes. You're paid per piece of content: a video, a post or a review. We agree what you make and the price with you, before you start.

Is the app live yet?

Not yet. Saigenai is being built right now, in public. Founding partners get early access before launch.

What do I have to commit to?

Nothing, until we agree the details. Once you start, we ask three things:

  • Use the app for real before you talk about it.
  • Mark paid posts as an ad (for example "#ad" or "Paid partnership"), as the law requires.
  • No fake results, no health or weight-loss promises, nothing that misleads people.
Who can apply?

Creators and coaches aged 18 or older. Each email address can apply once.
